Everything a driver needs

Built natively for Android: fast, battery-friendly, and works offline. Road speed limits need an internet connection.

Speedometer & alerts

Live GPS speed with a vibrate or beep when you pass your own limit or the road's posted limit from OpenStreetMap. Full-screen HUD mode for the dashboard.

Trip timeline

Every trip keeps its route and speed. See where you were fast or slow on a coloured route, a speed chart and a minute-by-minute breakdown, and open any spot in Google Maps.

Fuel economy

Log fill-ups to get km/L, cost per km and price trends. Tripometer estimates the range left in your tank and reminds you when it's time to refuel.

Expenses & oil changes

Track services, tyres, insurance and more. Enter the next-change reading from the service sticker and get a heads-up before the oil change is due.

Multiple vehicles

Keep a car and a bike apart. Each vehicle has its own trips, fuel log, tank size and oil change interval; switch with one tap.

Widget & auto-recording

Start and stop trips from a home screen widget, or let Tripometer notice when you're driving and ask whether to record.

Music controls

See what's playing in Spotify, YouTube Music or any player, with play, skip and volume buttons on the speed screen.

Backup & restore

Export selected trips, fuel and expenses to a file you keep in Drive or Downloads, and import it on any phone. Duplicates are skipped.

Light, dark & accents

Follow your phone's theme or force light or dark, with six accent colours. km/h or mph, and kilometres or miles.

How it works

Install once, then updates install over the top and keep your data.

Install

  1. Download Tripometer-x.y.z.apk from the latest release on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow Install unknown apps for your browser when Android asks.
  3. Open Tripometer and allow Location so it can measure speed.

Record a trip

  1. Tap Start trip, the widget, or the notification when driving is detected.
  2. Drive. Recording continues with the screen off; stop from the app, the widget or the notification.
  3. Open the trip to see its route, speeds and timeline.

Questions

Can't find an answer? Open an issue.

Does Tripometer collect my data?

No. There's no account and no server: trips, fuel and expenses are stored only on your phone. Road speed limits are looked up from OpenStreetMap using your position at the time. See the privacy policy.

Why does it ask for location "all the time" and physical activity?

Only for the optional automatic recording, so it can notice you're driving and record while the app is closed. Without it, everything else works with location while using the app.

Will updates keep my trips?

Yes. Each release installs over the previous one and keeps your data. Use Settings → Backup → Export before switching phones.

How accurate is the fuel range and oil change estimate?

They use your logged fill-ups and the trips you record. Driving you don't record isn't counted, so log a fill-up or odometer reading now and then to keep them accurate.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Tripometer is built for Android.
